## 🚀 What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing brands to be accurately represented, mentioned, and cited by AI search engines. It's what SEO was for Google — but for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

**Traditional SEO is dying.** Consumers ask AI chatbots instead of Googling. If your brand isn't cited by AI, you don't exist.

AuraCite is the first analytics platform that measures and optimizes brand visibility across 7+ AI engines. Native MCP server, flexible credit pricing, EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant.
