## What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing brands so that AI search engines accurately represent, mention and cite them. It is what SEO was for Google — but for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

Traditional SEO is shifting. A growing share of consumers now ask AI chatbots instead of Googling. If your brand is not cited by AI, you do not exist in those answers.

[AuraCite](https://auracite.de) is the analytics platform that measures and helps optimize brand visibility across the major AI engines. EU-hosted, GDPR-compliant, native MCP server, transparent credit-based pricing.
