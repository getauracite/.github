## What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimizing brands to be accurately represented, mentioned, and cited by AI search engines. It is what SEO was for Google, but for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

AI assistants increasingly influence research and buying journeys. If a brand is missing or described inaccurately in AI answers, teams need a way to detect and improve that visibility.

AuraCite measures brand visibility across selected AI engines, with citation tracking, competitor detection, MCP-ready workflows, visible credit usage, and EU hosting.
